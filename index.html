Start with one workshop. Stay for the habit.

Most craft workshops end when the session does. Craft Commons is built so you keep repairing and remaking what you already own, whether you join online from anywhere in India or in person in your city.

    A 4-week cohort for ₹2,999. One artisan-led session a week to mend, upcycle and remake clothes, textiles and home pieces you already own.

    ₹1,500 a year for 15% off every session, first access to new dates, monthly swap meetups and a next project after every session.

    Questions

    Is the membership worth it?

    Members pay ₹1,500 a year and get 15% off every workshop and cohort, early access to new dates, monthly swap meetups and make-togethers, and a next-project guide after every session. If you plan to keep crafting after your first workshop, it adds up quickly.

    Do I need any experience?

    No. Sessions marked Beginner assume you've never tried the craft. Each session lists its level.

    What do I need to bring?

    An item you already own that you'd like to repair or remake. We provide the tools, blocks, dyes and thread. When you book, tell us what you're bringing and we'll confirm it works for the session.

    Can I cancel or reschedule?

    Message us at least 48 hours before the session for a full refund or a move to another date. Within 48 hours, we can move you to another date if a seat is free.

    Are sessions suitable for children?

    Sessions marked Kids welcome are open to ages 8 and up with an accompanying adult.

    How do I pay?

    After you send your booking on WhatsApp, we reply with payment details. Your seat is held for 24 hours while you pay.
